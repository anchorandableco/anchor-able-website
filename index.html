IHSS

I sat through assessments without knowing what the county was really looking for. Now I know — and it's in these two guides.

In short: Most families are under-assessed because they don't document needs in the language IHSS uses. Keep a two-week log of everything you help with — every prompt, every reminder, every nighttime check. And if your child can't be left alone safely, ask about Protective Supervision: it's the service worth the most hours, and most families never hear it exists.

ABLE Accounts

Nobody told us we could save for my daughter without risking her SSI. This is the tool I wish we'd had from the start.

In short: Normally SSI cuts off when countable resources go over $2,000 — but money in an ABLE account (up to $100,000) doesn't count. It's the single most underused tool I see.

Good to Know

More good to know

Who is this guidance for?

It is for disabled people, parents, caregivers, representative payees, conservators, and family members who want a clearer way to understand benefit processes and prepare for the next step.

Is Anchor & Able Guide Co. a law firm?

No. Anchor & Able Guide Co. provides general educational information and practical process guidance, not legal advice or legal representation.

Does this replace official agency guidance?

No. Program rules can change and individual facts matter. Always confirm current requirements with the administering agency or a qualified professional before making a financial or legal decision.

What should I do with an urgent deadline?

Read the notice for appeal or response instructions, keep a copy, and contact the agency or an appropriate legal-services provider promptly. Do not wait for general educational content when a deadline is running.
