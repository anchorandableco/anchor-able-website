Most guides are written after the fact, from a desk. This one is being written from inside the process. Start with the questions every parent asks first

What does a Regional Center do?

In California, Regional Centers coordinate services for people with developmental disabilities — autism, intellectual disability, cerebral palsy, and related conditions. They evaluate your child, build an Individual Program Plan around their needs, and connect you with funded services. A full Regional Center guide is coming soon.

How do I get my child into the Regional Center system?

Start by contacting the Regional Center that serves your zip code and requesting an intake assessment — you don’t need a referral. They’ll evaluate whether your child qualifies, and if so, assign a service coordinator. My upcoming Regional Center guide will walk through the intake step by step.
